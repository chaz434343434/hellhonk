HONK

3 EPISODES - 16 LEVELS - UP TO 3 GEESE
WINDOWS MAC (APPLE SILICON) MAC (INTEL)
No install and no Python needed. The game keeps itself up to date. Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon, "Processor: Intel" means Intel.

Hell has flooded the park pond. You are one very angry goose with a brass horn, an egg cannon and no plan to forgive anyone. HONK is a fast 90s-style shooter: keycards, secret walls, exploding barrels, a face in the status bar that gets roughed up as you take damage, and a honk-metal soundtrack.

WHAT'S INSIDE

3 episodes, 16 levels
Fowl Waters (the park pond), Downstream (docks, canals, waterworks, marsh) and The Roost of Ash (a burning sky-temple). Every episode ends in a boss fight.
Every episode has its own monsters
The pond has Quacklings, Fire Swans and Gander Brutes. Downstream brings Coot Riflemen, Kingfisher Snipers, Grebe Grenadiers and dash-charging Swan Knights. The Roost of Ash has exploding Cinder Wrens, Raven Hexers, Vulture Reapers and Phoenix Hens that rise again. Each episode ends with its own boss.
18 weapons, 6 per episode
Honker to MEGAHONK at the pond. Harpoon Gun, Bilge Blunderbuss, Rivet Gun, Depth Charger and Tidal Horn downstream. Ember Sling, Feather Fan, Ash Repeater, Comet Caller and Sunburst in the Roost of Ash.
Powerups
Rage Bread, the Golden Egg, rubber boots and a park map, plus teleporters and secrets in every level.
3-player co-op
Host or join over the network, even mid-level. Keys are shared, you respawn while the flock fights on, and the boss gets tougher. Windows and Mac can play together.
Auto-updates
New versions download in the background and install on the title screen.

HOW TO PLAY

Windows

  1. Download HellHonk.exe and put it in its own folder.
  2. Run it. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the exe isn't code-signed. Click More info → Run anyway.

Mac (Mac mini, MacBook, iMac)

  1. Download the zip for your Mac. It unzips to HellHonk.app.
  2. Drag HellHonk.app into Applications. This step is required for auto-updates.
  3. Open it. The first time, macOS will say it can't verify the app because it isn't from the App Store. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. After that it opens normally.

Pick START on the title screen. Finished levels unlock in the level picker, including levels you beat in co-op.

CO-OP WITH A FRIEND

  1. Everyone needs the same version. The auto-updater keeps you matched.
  2. The host picks a level, then CO-OP: HOST GAME. The screen shows the host's IP address. Allow it through the firewall if asked.
  3. Friends pick CO-OP: JOIN GAME, type that IP and press Enter. Up to 3 geese can play, and a late friend can join a level already in progress.

On the same Wi-Fi it just works. Across the internet, both install a free VPN like Tailscale, Radmin VPN or ZeroTier and use the IP it gives the host. The other option is forwarding TCP port 29331 on the host's router.
